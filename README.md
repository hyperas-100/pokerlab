# PokerLab

Practice No-Limit Hold'em against bots with 25 different playstyles. After every hand you get a review of each of your decisions.

Static site, no build step, no accounts, nothing is sent anywhere. Open `index.html` or host it on GitHub Pages.

## Features

- Custom table: 2 to 9 players, your starting position (or random), stack depth, a playstyle for every seat, presets like "Soft game" and "Tough regs"
- Optional: fixed position drills (the button does not rotate), stacks reset every hand
- 25 bot playstyles (Rock, Solid Reg, Aggro Reg, Calling Station, Maniac, Slowplayer, Short-Stack Shover, Steamer that tilts, The Shark that adapts to you, and more) with VPIP / PFR / aggression stats measured by simulation
- Live HUD stats per opponent (VPIP / PFR), or reveal their playstyle outright
- Hand review after every hand:
  - Quick tips: opening charts by position plus pot odds
  - Deep analysis: Monte Carlo equity against ranges estimated from what each opponent did, EV of fold / call / several raise sizes, a grade for each decision (Best, Good, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Blunder), and a short explanation
  - Grade against these specific bots, or against a typical player
- Session screen: results, accuracy, EV given up by street and position, biggest mistakes, hand history

## How the review works (and its limits)

Opponent ranges start from their preflop action and position, then get narrowed on each street from their bets, calls and checks. Your equity is simulated against those ranges. Raises are valued with a fold-equity model that assumes opponents continue with their strongest hands. EV numbers are approximations, great for comparing your options, but they are not solver output.

## Files

- `engine.js` rules, hand evaluator, betting, side pots
- `ranks.js` starting-hand ranking and equity simulation
- `bots.js` the 25 playstyles and their decision logic
- `analysis.js` the review engine
- `stats.js` measured stats per style (generated by `test/styles.test.js`)
- `app.js`, `style.css`, `index.html` the interface

Tests live in `test/` and run with Node (`node test/engine.test.js`, `node test/botfuzz.test.js`, `node test/review.test.js`).
